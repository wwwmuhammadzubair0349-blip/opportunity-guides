What is this opportunity?

As a student or professional looking to advance your education, a fully funded PhD in the UK can be a life-changing opportunity. The UK is renowned for its academic excellence, and with the right funding, you can focus on your research without financial burdens.

You can apply for these opportunities regardless of your field of study or current level of education, from Bachelor's to PhD. However, some scholarships may have specific requirements or restrictions, so it's essential to review each opportunity carefully.

Benefits — what you actually get

  • Full tuition fee waiver
  • Living stipend (£15,000-£20,000 per year)
  • Travel costs
  • Research costs
  • Opportunity to study at a world-renowned university
  • Networking opportunities with academics and professionals
  • Career advancement opportunities

Eligibility — can you apply?

  • Be a citizen of an eligible country
  • Hold a Bachelor's or Master's degree
  • Meet the language proficiency requirements (English)
  • Meet the academic requirements (e.g., GPA, grades)
  • Not have previously studied in the UK
  • Be available to start the PhD program in the upcoming academic year
  • Meet the specific requirements of the scholarship or university

Documents you need to prepare

  • Passport💡 Ensure your passport is valid for at least two years
  • Transcripts💡 Get your transcripts translated and certified if they are not in English
  • Language proficiency certificate (e.g., IELTS, TOEFL)💡 Check the required score and take the test in advance
  • Personal statement💡 Tailor your statement to each scholarship and university
  • Research proposal💡 Ensure your proposal is well-structured and aligns with the university's research areas
  • References💡 Choose referees who know you well and can speak to your academic abilities
  • CV💡 Highlight your relevant skills, experience, and achievements
  • Proof of funding💡 Ensure you have sufficient funds for living expenses, if required

How to apply — step by step

  1. Choose the right programResearch the universities and programs that align with your interests and goals. Consider factors such as research areas, faculty expertise, and location. Make a list of potential programs and prioritize them based on your preferences.Pro tip: Contact the university's admissions team or faculty members to ask questions and get a better understanding of the program
  2. Prepare your documentsGather all the required documents and ensure they are in the correct format. Use the tips provided above to get each document right. Give yourself plenty of time to collect and prepare your documents, as some may take several weeks to obtain.Pro tip: Use a checklist to keep track of the documents you need to submit
  3. Check the online portalMost universities and scholarships have an online portal where you can submit your application. Check the portal for each opportunity and familiarize yourself with the application process. Create an account and start your application well in advance of the deadline.
    🔗 Open the University of Edinburgh portalPro tip: Make sure you have a strong internet connection and a compatible browser
  4. Write your essays and SOPYour personal statement and research proposal are crucial components of your application. Take your time to craft well-structured and compelling essays that showcase your skills, experience, and goals. Get feedback from others to improve your writing.Pro tip: Use specific examples to demonstrate your skills and achievements
  5. Secure your referencesChoose referees who know you well and can speak to your academic abilities. Give them plenty of time to write and submit their references. Provide them with your CV, personal statement, and research proposal to help them write a strong reference.Pro tip: Send reminders to your referees to ensure they submit their references on time
  6. Submit your applicationOnce you have completed all the steps above, submit your application through the online portal. Ensure you have uploaded all the required documents and paid any application fees. Review your application carefully before submitting it.
    🔗 Submit your Gates Cambridge applicationPro tip: Take a screenshot of your submitted application as proof of submission
  7. After submissionAfter submitting your application, you will typically receive an acknowledgement email. If you do not receive an email, contact the admissions team to confirm receipt of your application. You can also track the status of your application through the online portal.Pro tip: Follow up with the admissions team if you have not received a decision within the expected timeframe
  8. Prepare for an interview (if required)Some scholarships or universities may require an interview as part of the application process. Prepare for the interview by researching the university and the scholarship, practicing your responses to common questions, and preparing any materials you may need (e.g., CV, research proposal).Pro tip: Use the STAR method to structure your responses to behavioral questions

Mistakes that get applications rejected

  • Not meeting the eligibility criteria
  • Submitting an incomplete application
  • Not tailoring your application to each scholarship or university
  • Not getting feedback on your essays and SOP
  • Not giving your referees enough time to write and submit their references
  • Not proofreading your application for errors

Tips to stand out from thousands of applicants

  • Start your application process early to avoid last-minute rush
  • Tailor your application to each scholarship and university
  • Showcase your unique skills and experiences
  • Demonstrate your passion and commitment to your field
  • Get feedback from others to improve your application
  • Use specific examples to demonstrate your skills and achievements
  • Proofread your application carefully to avoid errors

FAQ

What is the deadline for the Chevening Scholarship?

The deadline for the Chevening Scholarship usually opens around August and closes in November. Check the official website for the exact deadline.

Can I apply for multiple scholarships at the same time?

Yes, you can apply for multiple scholarships at the same time. However, ensure you meet the eligibility criteria for each scholarship and tailor your application accordingly.

Do I need to have a confirmed supervisor before applying for a PhD program?

It is not always necessary to have a confirmed supervisor before applying for a PhD program. However, it is recommended that you contact potential supervisors to discuss your research proposal and get feedback.

Can I apply for a PhD program with a lower GPA?

Some universities may consider applicants with a lower GPA, but it depends on the specific program and university. Check the eligibility criteria for each program and contact the admissions team if you have any questions.

How long does it take to hear back after submitting my application?

The time it takes to hear back after submitting your application varies depending on the university and scholarship. Typically, you can expect to hear back within 2-6 months after the deadline.
